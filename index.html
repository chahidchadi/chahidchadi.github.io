Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at Moulay Ismail University. I am a member of the Laboratoire Analyse Mathématique et Numérique des EDP et Applications (AMNEA), advised by Professor Lahmi Badr and Professor Khallouq Samir. My research focuses on solving Partial Differential Equations using Parallel Physics-Informed Neural Networks via Domain Decomposition methods (PINNs).

Before my doctoral studies, I completed my Master's degree in Numerical Analysis of Partial Differential Equations (ANEDP) at Moulay Ismail University, My Master's thesis focused on "Schwarz-type domain decomposition methods" under the guidance of Professor Khallouq Samir. I also hold a Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics from the same institution, where I worked with Professor Mohamed Zitane on "Solving differential equations using Laplace transform."
